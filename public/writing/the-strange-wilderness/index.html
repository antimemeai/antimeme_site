The Strange Wilderness

c. May 12, 2024 · note

Polycrisis, upheaval, paradigm shift--call it what you want, the world is changing. People debate the details but most, I think, agree that the world today is changing at a far more rapid pace than it has in the recent past. My personal view is that we've hit upon several massive events, each of which are world-shaking by themselves, all at once:

  • The end of the Post-War Era, specifically Pax Americana, and with it globalization.
  • The dawn of generative AI, which will at least disrupt, if not shatter, our conception of truth, the very relationship between senses and belief.
  • An Accelerationist-Revolutionary-Reactionary time bomb ticking across the globe, putting existential pressure on the concept of "nation".
  • Social, cultural, and political reshuffling at scale as both a serious dynamic in its own right and as connective tissue between other elements.
  • Beginning to reap various aspects of the Anthropocene, not the least of which is our changing climate.

If you don't like that formulation, substitute your own version. Regardless, some flavor of that is the world that you work in, that you are trying to build your life in, that companies, communities, and states must adapt to mid-flight to survive.

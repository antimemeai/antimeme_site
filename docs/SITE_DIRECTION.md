# Site direction

Operator direction, 2026-10-17. Read alongside README before changing copy or
structure. This supersedes conflicting proposals in NOTE_FROM_BUSINESS.md;
that colleague's note is input, not an approved specification.

## Voice

Iceberg Hemingway: know the larger picture; reveal a few concrete, intriguing
parts. Do not explain our sophistication or narrate a persona. Dry, specific,
slightly amused, not self-important or mysterious for effect. Preserve the
range from technical judgment and leadership to hands-on work without a long
account of why those belong together.

## What this site is

A shingle for independent, bespoke technical work. Each engagement has its own
shape and window. Neither a repeatable package nor a unit of the operator's
time is the product. Do not infer a standard duration, pricing model, staffing
plan, or growth strategy. Advisory work is not excluded by this distinction.
The capability examples are glimpses of possible work, not a complete catalog.

No funnel. No packages. No explanation of how engagements work. No discovery /
SOW / retainer sequence, timelines, public prices, or justification of fees.
The proposed engagement section in the business note is explicitly rejected.
The invitation is email. Do not add conversion machinery or an agency-growth
story. Private calibration and personal ambitions are not public copy.

## Space and implementation

One screen where the viewport has sufficient room. New material must earn
space, not automatically lengthen the page. Small viewports and enlarged text
may scroll; never hide content to force a fit. Keep the static wordmark, local
fonts, plain HTML/CSS, and keyboard access. No animations, analytics, badges,
frameworks, or build step.

The operator subsequently requested top navigation and a separate open-source
page. Keep it a short software page, not a resume: concrete descriptions,
honest maturity, working source links. The initial entries are Blackbird,
Verbs, and BRepGPU, whose existing GitHub repositories are publicly accessible.
The operator also requested SALib and crate links for SALib and Verbs.
Link these existing repositories and published crates without declaring a new
canonical hosting policy. Do not list a local-only or unreleased project as
available software.

No service reclassification, hosting strategy, ownership statement, or
licensing policy is adopted merely because the business note proposes it.
Decide those separately with the operator. Do not remove concrete capabilities
just to make the page look like a conventional consultancy.

On 2026-10-21 the operator requested a Writing tab and a simple list of four
recovered essays. Keep the index restrained. Essays use a readable text column
and scroll naturally, with previous/next links at the top and bottom only
where a neighbor exists. Preserve the substantive essay text and formatting;
links between the imported essays point to their local pages. The operator
subsequently requested removal of all preamble content except the essay titles:
start directly at the opening substantive section. Previous/next links use the
same quiet monospace style as the main navigation, with destination titles in
accessible labels rather than displayed alongside the arrows.
